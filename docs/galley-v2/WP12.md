# WP12 auto-pairs

## Summary
Feature 12's auto-closing now works in the Source editor, and each behaviour has its own switch under Settings › Typing. All switches are on by default; under the legacy specs' `automatic-off` they start off.
- Brackets: `( [ {` also close before `$ . , = \ ' ~ &` and other punctuation.
- Math: `$` gives `$¦$`, and a second `$` gives `$$¦$$`. `\(` and `\[` get `\)` and `\]`. In math, `\{` gets `\}` and `\left(`/`\bigl[`/`\left.`/`\left\{` get their `\right…`/`\bigr…` closer. In text, `\{` gets no closer.
- Quotes: a backtick gives `` `¦' ``, and two give ``` ``¦'' ```.
- Typing over: typing a closer the editor added, or typing it out in full (`\)`, `\right)`, `''`), steps over it. This includes braces a completion inserted, so `\textb`, Tab, `old}` gives `\textbf{old}¦`.
- Backspace inside a new LaTeX pair removes it, and `$$¦$$` goes back to `$¦$`.
- Tab steps out of a tracked closer.
- Enter inside `\[¦\]` splits it onto three lines.
- `$5 ` becomes `\$5 `, as one undoable automatic edit that Backspace reverts.

With every switch off, the editor types exactly as Version 1 did.

## Deviations
- **`applyCommand` is in `latexExtras.ts`, which WP12 does not own.** So completion braces are tracked generically, in `closerField`: every local `input.complete` transaction tracks the `)`, `]` and `}` it inserted after the caret, on the caret's line. That covers `applyCommand` (`\textbf{}`, `\frac{}{}`), macro completions and package snippets without editing `latexExtras.ts`. `applyPath` also dispatches `trackClosers` itself, as the plan says.
- **closeBrackets' own pairs are tracked too.** A local `input.type` that inserts exactly `()`, `[]` or `{}` with the caret inside is tracked, so Tab can step out of a `{` the person typed. This happens only while the "brackets" or "Tab" switch is on.
- **Visual mode:** only the bracket behaviour (wider closing, type-over, Tab out) and the currency guard run there. The `$`, delimiter and quote pairs run only in Source mode, because Visual draws formulas and quotes itself.
- **Brackets off keeps V1's closeBrackets.** The plan says "each switch off restores today's behaviour", so the switch never removes `( [ {` closing. It removes only the wider `before` list and the tracking of completion braces.
- **Setting ids:** `pairs.brackets`, `pairs.math`, `pairs.quotes`, `pairs.tabOut` and `pairs.currency`. All are account scope, section `typing`, `automatic: true` and default true. `brackets` and `math` are `palette: true`.

## For later packages
- **Closers (WP31, WP40, anyone inserting a closer):**
  - Dispatch `trackClosers.of([{ from, to, text, openerEnd?, open? }])` from `editor/core/brackets.ts`. The closer then gets type-over, Tab out and, with `open`/`openerEnd`, Backspace.
  - `closerAt(state, pos)` reads them.
  - A tracked closer is dropped once the caret leaves its line or its text changes.
- **Tab yields:** Tab 72 (`pairs.stepOut`) runs only when a tracked closer is right after the caret.
- **Enter:** Enter 90 (`pairs.splitDisplay`) acts only on `\[¦\]`. Environment-argument jumps at Enter 90 (WP31) need a different condition, and the chain's tie-break is manifest order.
- **Exports for later packages:** `completions.ts` exports `readSources`, `PATH_RULES`, `PathRule`, `relativePath` and `macroNamesNow`.
